Welcome To Tapps Island Golf

Tapps Island Golf Course, one of Washington’s most scenic and inviting places to play. Nestled along the beautiful shores of Lake Tapps, our course offers stunning waterfront views, well-maintained fairways, and a relaxing atmosphere for golfers of all skill levels. Whether you’re here for a competitive round or a casual day with friends, we’re excited to have you and hope you enjoy everything our course has to offer.
